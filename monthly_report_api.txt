The Monthly Reports screen (`reports_screen.dart`) generates all of its data natively on the app by calculating stats from the Transactions API. 

Here is the exact API used to populate the charts, statistics, and history on that screen:

API Endpoint:
GET `baseurl/vendors/transactions`

Dart Method: 
`getTransactions(String token)` in `api_service.dart`

How it works:
The app fetches your entire transaction history from this API. When you select a specific month (like "April 2026"), the app filters the transactions for that month, and then manually adds up the `final_amount` and counts the number of jobs per day to draw the Earnings Trend and Earnings Distribution charts!
